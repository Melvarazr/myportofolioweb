Overview

Project Description

BAS Hiring is an integrated recruitment management system designed to streamline and optimize the hiring process for PT Barokah Amanah Sentosa. This system automates recruitment workflows, including account management, candidate application processing, job posting, and outsourcing service requests. The platform enables recruiters, general managers, and candidates to interact efficiently through role-based dashboards, notifications, and real-time updates.

Capabilities

Key Features

Account Management

Admin can manage user accounts and role permissions, ensuring only valid users access the system.

Job Application

Candidates can apply for jobs through a seamless registration and application tracking process.

Executive & Operational Dashboard

Custom dashboards for General Managers, Admins, and Recruiters to monitor hiring metrics in real-time.

Notifications

Real-time notifications for candidates and vendors regarding their application or service request status.

Job Postings

Recruiters can publish, update, and manage open job listings and incoming candidate pipelines.

Outsourcing Services

Dedicated vendor integration to submit, review, and manage corporate outsourcing requests.
