Context

Background

M-Tix is Cinema 21's official mobile app for booking movie tickets online, launched in 2015 to give customers an easy way to buy tickets without waiting in line at theaters. Despite having many app downloads, the company noticed that most users weren't completing their first ticket purchase, which meant lost revenue and wasted marketing efforts.

Challenge

Problem Statement

M-Tix had a significant activation bottleneck — while app downloads grew 55.4% from February to March 2024, paid users only grew 14%, with the conversion rate dropping from 0.58% to 0.43%. Most users were downloading the app but never buying their first ticket.

Objective

Project Goal

Increase the number of new users who complete their first ticket purchase (activation rate) by identifying and fixing barriers in the user journey that prevent users from converting from app installers to paying customers. Led a complete product management plan to streamline onboarding and payment flows.
