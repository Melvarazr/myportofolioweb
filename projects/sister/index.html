Overview

Project Description

ClimED is a mobile application designed to increase public awareness on climate change and air quality issues. The app empowers users to monitor real-time air quality (AQI), track their personal carbon footprint, access educational articles, and connect with local environmental communities. ClimED delivers an inclusive and intuitive experience for all user segments, from environmental enthusiasts to everyday commuters.

Capabilities

Key Features

Real-time Air Quality Monitoring

View up-to-date AQI and PM2.5 data for any location, complete with detailed explanations and actionable health recommendations.

Carbon Emission Calculator (EcoCalc)

Track personal carbon footprint from daily activities such as commuting, shopping, and household electricity usage.

GeoCarbon Insights

Explore carbon emission statistics by country and year, visualized through clean interactive charts.

Environmental Community Hub

Discover, join, and donate to environmental organizations, with quick bookmarking for favorite communities.

Educational Articles

Access and save curated educational content on sustainability topics, including a dedicated “Liked Articles” library.

Design Workflow

Process Overview

01

User Research & Persona Development

Conducted interviews with 6 target users to identify pain points, motivations, and expectations regarding air quality monitoring and eco-friendly lifestyles.

02

Journey Mapping & Value Proposition

Created user journey maps and value proposition canvas to define key flows and product vision.

03

Information Architecture & Wireframing

Built intuitive site maps and low-fidelity wireframes to organize main features and user flows.

04

High-Fidelity Prototyping

Developed interactive prototypes in Figma, focusing on accessibility, visual hierarchy, and user-centric design.

05

Usability Testing & Iteration

Ran usability tests and collected System Usability Scale (SUS) feedback to refine navigation, clarify feature names, and improve visual clarity.
