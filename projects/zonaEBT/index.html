ZonaEBT REC Calculator: Simplifying Renewable Energy Certificate Calculation

Developed a complete product management solution to simplify Renewable Energy Certificate (REC) calculation and purchasing by translating business and user needs into a transparent, user-friendly digital experience supporting corporate ESG goals.

Context

Overview

Renewable Energy Certificates (REC) play an important role in helping companies achieve their sustainability and ESG goals. However, estimating REC requirements and understanding the certification process can be challenging due to complex calculations and limited user-friendly digital tools. Existing solutions often focus on either energy simulation or certificate purchasing, requiring users to switch between different platforms. As part of my Product Owner internship at ZonaEBT, I worked on developing a REC Calculator that simplifies REC calculation, provides transparent cost estimates, and streamlines the certificate purchasing process through a single platform.

Objective

Goals

The goal of this project was to design a simple and transparent REC Calculator that enables companies to calculate REC requirements, estimate costs, and purchase official certificates through a single platform. By simplifying the overall process, the product improves user confidence and encourages greater corporate adoption of renewable energy solutions.

Product Lifecycle

Process Overview

01

Product Discovery

Defined the product opportunity by analyzing business challenges, validating assumptions through hypotheses, conducting competitor analysis, and identifying user needs through personas to shape the MVP product vision.

02

Product Planning

Translated research findings into a clear product vision, defined the MVP scope, and prioritized features based on business objectives and user value to support ZonaEBT's goals.

03

Requirement Definition

Created structured user stories and acceptance criteria to transform business and user needs into actionable product requirements for design and engineering collaboration.

04

User Flow Design

Designed the end-to-end user journey covering electricity consumption input, instant REC calculation review, certificate selection, and checkout completion.

05

Prototype Collaboration

Collaborated closely with the UI/UX Designer to refine product requirements throughout prototyping, ensuring alignment between user needs and business objectives prior to development.
